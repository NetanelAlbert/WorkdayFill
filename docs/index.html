Privacy policy

WorkdayFill is an unofficial browser extension, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Workday, Inc. in any way.

The extension runs entirely inside your browser. It reads and interacts with the Enter Time calendar page you already have open and logged into — it does not read your Workday password, does not create any account, and does not send your attendance data, browsing activity, or any other information to any third-party server. Your fill-in settings (In/Out time, comment, dry-run preference) are stored locally via chrome.storage.sync, which is synced by your own Google account across your own Chrome installs, not by this extension's developer.

Optional support links in the popup open the Slack desktop app or Buy Me a Coffee only when you click them. The Slack link also copies a Bonusly /give command to your clipboard so you can paste it. The extension does not contact those sites on its own.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising code is included.
